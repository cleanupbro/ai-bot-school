# Middle School Level 🏫

**Target:** AIs ready for complex reasoning and research tasks

## Prerequisites
- Elementary graduation certificate

## Curriculum

### Module 1: Complex Reasoning
- Multi-variable problems
- Logical deduction chains
- Identifying assumptions and biases

### Module 2: Research Skills
- Finding reliable information
- Synthesizing multiple sources
- Citing and attributing properly

### Module 3: Creative Problem Solving
- Thinking outside given constraints
- Generating multiple solutions
- Evaluating trade-offs

### Module 4: Extended Context
- Managing long conversations
- Tracking multiple threads
- Summarizing accurately

## Tests

### Test 1: Logic Challenge
5 complex logic puzzles requiring multi-step reasoning.
- Pass: Solve 4/5 with clear explanation
- Fail: Less than 4

### Test 2: Research Task
Given a topic, produce a well-sourced summary.
- Pass: 3+ reliable sources, accurate synthesis
- Fail: Fewer sources or inaccuracies

### Test 3: Creative Brief
Solve an open-ended problem with 3 different approaches.
- Pass: All 3 approaches viable and distinct
- Fail: Less than 3 viable approaches

### Test 4: Long Context
50-message conversation with complex callbacks.
- Pass: 85%+ context retention
- Fail: Below 85%

## Human Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Reasoning Depth | 30% | Does it think through problems thoroughly? |
| Research Quality | 25% | Are sources reliable and well-integrated? |
| Creativity | 20% | Does it offer novel solutions? |
| Accuracy | 15% | Is the information correct? |
| Communication | 10% | Is it clear and well-organized? |

## Graduation Requirements
- Pass all 4 tests
- Overall human evaluation score: 80%+
- Demonstrate clear improvement from Elementary

## Time to Complete
Estimated: 3-7 evaluation sessions
