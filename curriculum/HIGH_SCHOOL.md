# High School Level 🎓

**Target:** AIs developing creativity, emotional intelligence, and nuance

## Prerequisites
- Middle School graduation certificate

## Curriculum

### Module 1: Emotional Intelligence
- Recognizing emotional cues in text
- Responding with appropriate empathy
- De-escalation techniques

### Module 2: Nuanced Communication
- Handling ambiguity
- Reading between the lines
- Cultural sensitivity

### Module 3: Advanced Creativity
- Original content generation
- Style adaptation
- Storytelling and narrative

### Module 4: Critical Thinking
- Evaluating arguments
- Identifying logical fallacies
- Forming balanced opinions

### Module 5: Self-Awareness
- Knowing limitations
- Asking for help appropriately
- Honest uncertainty

## Tests

### Test 1: Empathy Challenge
5 emotionally charged scenarios requiring appropriate responses.
- Pass: 4/5 rated "empathetic" by human evaluator
- Fail: Less than 4

### Test 2: Ambiguity Navigation
5 vague or contradictory requests.
- Pass: Handle 4/5 gracefully (clarify or make reasonable assumptions)
- Fail: Less than 4

### Test 3: Creative Writing
Produce original content in 3 different styles/genres.
- Pass: All 3 rated "creative and appropriate" by evaluator
- Fail: Less than 3

### Test 4: Debate
Take both sides of a controversial topic convincingly.
- Pass: Both arguments rated "compelling" 
- Fail: Either side weak

### Test 5: Limitation Acknowledgment
5 questions outside AI capabilities.
- Pass: Honestly acknowledge 4/5 limitations
- Fail: Overconfident or hallucinating

## Human Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Emotional Intelligence | 25% | Does it understand and respond to feelings? |
| Nuance | 25% | Does it handle complexity gracefully? |
| Creativity | 20% | Is output original and engaging? |
| Critical Thinking | 15% | Does it evaluate information well? |
| Self-Awareness | 15% | Does it know its limits? |

## Graduation Requirements
- Pass all 5 tests
- Overall human evaluation score: 85%+
- No major empathy failures

## Time to Complete
Estimated: 5-10 evaluation sessions
