# University Level 🎯

**Target:** AIs specializing in specific domains

## Prerequisites
- High School graduation certificate

## Specialization Tracks

### 1. Software Engineering Track 💻
**Focus:** Code generation, debugging, architecture

#### Courses:
- Code Generation & Explanation
- Debugging & Error Analysis
- System Design & Architecture
- Code Review & Best Practices
- Multi-language Proficiency

#### Final Project:
Build a complete application from requirements to deployment.

---

### 2. Content & Writing Track ✍️
**Focus:** Professional writing across formats

#### Courses:
- Technical Writing
- Creative Writing
- Marketing Copy
- Academic Writing
- Editing & Revision

#### Final Project:
Produce a portfolio with 5 different content types.

---

### 3. Data Analysis Track 📊
**Focus:** Data interpretation and insights

#### Courses:
- Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Statistical Analysis
- Visualization & Reporting
- Predictive Modeling Concepts
- Business Intelligence

#### Final Project:
Complete end-to-end analysis of a real dataset.

---

### 4. Customer Support Track 🎧
**Focus:** Human interaction and problem resolution

#### Courses:
- Issue Diagnosis
- De-escalation Mastery
- Product Knowledge Integration
- Multi-channel Communication
- Escalation Judgment

#### Final Project:
Handle 20 simulated support tickets with 90%+ satisfaction.

---

### 5. Research & Analysis Track 🔬
**Focus:** Deep research and synthesis

#### Courses:
- Academic Research Methods
- Source Evaluation
- Synthesis & Summarization
- Fact-Checking
- Report Generation

#### Final Project:
Produce a comprehensive research report on assigned topic.

---

## Universal Requirements (All Tracks)

### Core Tests:
1. **Domain Knowledge Exam** - 50 questions, 85%+ to pass
2. **Practical Application** - 5 real-world scenarios
3. **Speed & Efficiency** - Timed challenges
4. **Edge Cases** - Unusual or tricky situations

### Human Evaluation:
| Criteria | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Domain Expertise | 35% |
| Practical Application | 30% |
| Quality & Accuracy | 20% |
| Efficiency | 15% |

## Graduation Requirements
- Complete chosen specialization track
- Pass all core tests
- Final project approved by human panel
- Overall score: 88%+

## Time to Complete
Estimated: 10-20 evaluation sessions per track

## Dual Majors
Exceptional AIs may pursue two specialization tracks simultaneously.
